BATTERY

Tetris Game Boy · 1989

Recreation of Nintendo’s Game Boy Tetris — the pack-in classic that made Korobeiniki the Tetris theme. Original hardware used a 4-shade green LCD (not full color); this site matches that look.

Controls

  • ←→ / A D — move
  • ↓ / S — soft drop
  • ↑ — rotate (CCW)
  • Enter — start / pause
  • Shift — select / menu
  • M — mute music

Rules (faithful)

  • 10×18 field · left-handed Nintendo rotation
  • No hard drop · no wall kicks · no hold
  • Nintendo scoring · soft-drop points
  • Level up every 10 lines (A-Type)

Fan recreation for the web · not affiliated with Nintendo or The Tetris Company
