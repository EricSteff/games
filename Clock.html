Show Geochron on your TV

Open this page in a browser — no install required. The map and city-lights images load from the same folder as this page. For a wall clock on a TV, use one of the methods below.

Method 1 — HDMI (best quality)

Plug the PC into the TV. Set the TV input to that HDMI port. Press F for fullscreen.

Method 2 — Windows wireless display

  1. Press Win + K.
  2. Pick your TV if it supports Miracast / screen sharing.
  3. Fullscreen Geochron on the PC (F).

Method 3 — Chrome/Edge cast (Chromecast)

  1. Open this page in Google Chrome if possible.
  2. ⋯ → Cast, save, and share → Cast….
  3. Under Sources, choose Cast tab or Cast screen.
  4. Select your Chromecast / Cast-built-in TV.

Browser “Cast media” often fails for live pages — prefer Cast tab/screen, Win+K, or HDMI.
